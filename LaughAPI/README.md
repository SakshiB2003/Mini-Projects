# 😂 LaughAPI  
### Because everyone deserves a good laugh 🤡

**LaughAPI** is a cheerful little web app that serves **fresh, personalized jokes** straight from the internet.  
Just type your name, hit a button, and *boom!*—a joke with your name on it 😌✨

---

## 🌈 What’s This All About?

Ever felt like your day needed a tiny serotonin boost?  
LaughAPI fetches jokes from **JokeAPI** and personalizes them just for *you*.  
No sign-ups. No drama. Just laughs. 😄
